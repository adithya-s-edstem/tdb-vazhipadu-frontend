# P5 – Mobile and accessibility

Branch: `feature/p5-mobile-and-accessibility`

Most devotees use a phone, and the board's apps must be accessible.

## Requirements

- The offerings list, cart and order form work well on a 360 px wide phone screen as well as on a desktop.
- The whole booking flow works with the keyboard alone.
- Every form field has a proper label, and errors are announced to screen reader users.
- Colours have enough contrast to read (WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body text).

## Acceptance criteria

- No horizontal scrolling at 360 px wide.
- A test or a written keyboard walkthrough in the PR shows the flow done without a mouse.
- Tests find form fields by their labels.
